Human-managed entry doesn't scale — or protect anyone

Guardhouses and cashier counters still run on manual registration, physical cards, and guesswork. That means long queues, unidentified criminal or troublemaker faces, and membership discounts nobody can verify on the spot.

Human-managed

How it works

From face scan to verified entry, in four steps

Self-registration

Visitor or customer downloads the VisitorKu AI app and scans their face once.

AI face analytic

The centralized database validates the face against criminal, troublemaker, white-list and black-list records.

Rules-based approval

Management sets auto-approve, single-approve, or double-approve rules per site.

Entry & notify

Owner or staff is auto-notified via app the moment a face is detected and entry is granted.
