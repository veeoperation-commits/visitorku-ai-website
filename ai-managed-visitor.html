How it works

Self-entry, validated in real time

Download & register

Visitor downloads the VisitorKu AI app, scans their face and registers their car plate.

AI face analytic

The centralized database auto-validates against criminal / troublemaker / white-list / black-list face records.

Approval rules apply

Auto-approve, or route to owner / staff / operator for single- or double-layer approval via the app.

Entry & attendance

Barrier, door or lift opens; owner is notified; attendance is auto-recorded.

Integrated hardware

One face scan, three access points

Barrier gate

Detects visitor face and car plate together; opens on auto, one-layer or two-layer approval.

Access door

Detects visitor face and unlocks automatically — no card, no PIN, no queue.

Lift

Recognizes the face walking into the lobby and auto-calls the lift and presses the floor — no manual button.

Real-time criminal & stranger alerts

Detected criminal face, stranger face, or a helmet-obscured face without visibility triggers an instant "Alert Criminal Attack" notification to everyone on the app.
